Common questions

Before you apply

Is this really free?

Yes. There's no fee to register or attend the 1-week bootcamp. It costs ₦0.

Do I need previous experience?

No. The bootcamp is built for people starting from zero — what matters is that you show up and are willing to learn.

What if I don't have a laptop?

Bring yours if you have one. If not, a laptop and internet access can be provided for you to use during training.

What happens if I miss a day?

You need to attend at least 4 of the 5 days to complete the bootcamp, so one missed day is fine — try not to miss more than that.

Registering doesn't guarantee a spot?

That's right — only 6 participants are selected for this intake, out of everyone who applies. Apply early and answer the qualification questions honestly.

What happens after the bootcamp?

If you want to keep going, you can apply for the free Skill Completion + Structured Mentorship program, which has stricter attendance and participation requirements.
